# 01 — Notebook Information & Scope

<StickyNote color="yellow" title="Intrinsic Valuation Axiom">
  "Price is what you pay; value is what you get." — Warren Buffett. The intrinsic value of any commercial asset equals the present value of its future free cash flows discounted at a rate reflecting the risk profile of those flows.
</StickyNote>

* **Domain**: Finance & Capital Allocation
* **Subject**: Corporate Valuation
* **Core Reference Model**: Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) Discounting & Modigliani-Miller Theorem

# 02 — Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) Derivation

<FormulaCard
  title="FCFF Unlevered Cash Flow Equation"
  formula="\mathrm{FCFF} = \mathrm{EBIT}(1 - t) + \mathrm{D\&A} - \mathrm{CapEx} - \Delta \mathrm{NWC}"
  variables={[
    { symbol: "\\mathrm{EBIT}(1 - t)", description: "Net Operating Profit After Taxes (NOPAT)" },
    { symbol: "\\mathrm{D\\&A}", description: "Depreciation & Amortization non-cash tax shields added back" },
    { symbol: "\\mathrm{CapEx}", description: "Capital expenditures invested in fixed assets / property, plant & equipment" },
    { symbol: "\\Delta \\mathrm{NWC}", description: "Net change in non-cash Working Capital (Accounts Receivable + Inventory - Accounts Payable)" }
  ]}
  explanation="FCFF represents cash generated by core operations available to all capital providers (both debt holders and equity shareholders) after reinvestment in capital assets and working capital."
/>

# 03 — Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

<FormulaCard
  title="WACC Hurdle Rate Equation"
  formula="\mathrm{WACC} = \left(\frac{E}{V}\right) r_e + \left(\frac{D}{V}\right) r_d (1 - t)"
  variables={[
    { symbol: "E / V", description: "Proportion of equity in total enterprise market capitalization" },
    { symbol: "D / V", description: "Proportion of debt in total enterprise market capitalization" },
    { symbol: "r_e", description: "Cost of equity derived from CAPM: r_e = r_f + beta * (r_m - r_f)" },
    { symbol: "r_d (1 - t)", description: "After-tax cost of corporate debt (accounting for corporate tax interest shield)" }
  ]}
  explanation="Reflects the minimum expected hurdle rate of return demanded by the enterprise's combined capital providers."
/>

# 04 — Active Recall Flashcards & Conceptual Quiz

<Flashcard
  front="Why is interest expense NOT deducted when calculating Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)?"
  back="Because FCFF belongs to all capital providers (debt and equity). Interest expense is a return to debt holders and is accounted for in the discount rate (WACC), not in the cash flow numerator."
/>
