# 02 — Mintzberg's Five Organizational Configurations

Henry Mintzberg identified five archetypal organizational forms based on the dominant coordinating mechanism:

| Configuration | Dominant Coordinating Mechanism | Key Part of Organization | Environmental Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Simple Structure** | Direct Supervision | Strategic Apex (Founder / CEO) | Simple, dynamic, entrepreneurial |
| **Machine Bureaucracy** | Standardization of Work Processes | Technostructure (Planners, Engineers) | Simple, stable, high volume (e.g., auto assembly) |
| **Professional Bureaucracy**| Standardization of Skills | Operating Core (Surgeons, Professors) | Complex, stable, knowledge work (e.g., hospitals, universities) |
| **Divisionalized Form** | Standardization of Outputs | Middle Line (Business Unit Presidents)| Diversified markets, mature conglomerates |
| **Adhocracy (Innovative)** | Mutual Adjustment | Support Staff / Project Teams | Complex, dynamic, cutting-edge technology |
