# Integrative Manager for Analytics (GM 410)

Master study suite for the PGDM Research & Business Analytics programme at Welingkar Institute (WeSchool).

### Course Modules & Scope
* **Module 0**: Course Orientation, Grading Rubrics & Past Exam Trends
* **Module 1**: Strategic Intent, Vision/Mission, 5 Ps & Mintzberg Emergent Strategies
* **Module 2**: External Environmental Analysis, PESTEL & Porter's Five Forces
* **Module 3**: Internal Capabilities, Generic Strategies, VRIO & McKinsey 7S
* **Module 4**: Corporate Portfolio Management (BCG Growth-Share & GE-McKinsey 9-Box)
* **Module 5**: Blue Ocean Strategy, Value Innovation & Strategy Canvas (NVIDIA Case IN2027)
* **Module 6**: Technology for Business Strategy, Big Data 5 Vs, Analytics Hierarchy & AI
* **Module 7**: Sustainability, Dunphy's Phase Model, ESG Governance & Circular Economy
* **Module 8**: Case Study Master Library (NVIDIA 15-Question Matrix, Microsoft, Amazon, Coca-Cola)
* **Module 9**: Past Examination Master Bank (2023, 2024, 2025 Full Solutions)
