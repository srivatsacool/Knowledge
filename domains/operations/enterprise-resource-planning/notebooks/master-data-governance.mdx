# 02 — FOUNDATIONS: The Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

In legacy non-integrated corporate environments, customer and vendor information is duplicated across isolated sales, billing, shipping, and accounting spreadsheets. When a customer changes their corporate tax identifier or shipping address, one department updates its records while the others continue using obsolete data.

An integrated ERP enforces the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)**:
* Every physical entity in the enterprise exists exactly once in the master repository.
* When a record is updated, the modification propagates instantaneously to all operational processes.
* Eliminates interdepartmental reconciliation variances and ensures data integrity across global operations.

# 03 — CORE CONCEPTS: The Three Strata of Enterprise Data

Enterprise data divides into three distinct hierarchical strata:

| Data Stratum | Frequency of Modification | Core Business Purpose | Key Enterprise Examples | System Immutability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organizational Data** | Extremely Rare (Months/Years) | Defines enterprise legal and physical operational hierarchy | Client, Company Code, Plant, Storage Location, Sales Org | Fixed in system configuration tables |
| **Master Data** | Low (Weeks/Months) | Core business entities referenced repeatedly in transactions | Material Master, Customer Master (BP), Vendor Master, Chart of Accounts | Read heavily; strictly audited changes |
| **Transactional Data** | High (Real-Time Seconds) | Event records created during commercial execution | Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts, Invoices | Immutable once posted (Document Principle) |

### String Metric Formulation for Fuzzy Record Matching
In Master Data deduplication, similarity between two legacy string records $s_1$ and $s_2$ is evaluated using normalized edit distance:

<FormulaCard
  title="Normalized Levenshtein Similarity Metric"
  formula="\mathrm{Sim}(s_1, s_2) = 1 - \frac{d_L(s_1, s_2)}{\max(|s_1|, |s_2|)}"
  variables={[
    { symbol: "\\mathrm{Sim}(s_1, s_2)", description: "Normalized string similarity coefficient bounded between 0.0 (disjoint) and 1.0 (exact match)" },
    { symbol: "d_L(s_1, s_2)", description: "Levenshtein distance (minimum number of single-character insertions, deletions, or substitutions required to transform s1 into s2)" },
    { symbol: "|s_1|, |s_2|", description: "Character lengths of string 1 and string 2" }
  ]}
  units="Dimensionless ratio [0.00, 1.00]"
  assumptions="1. Strings are pre-processed to lowercase. 2. Punctuation and common corporate suffixes (Inc, LLC, Corp) are stripped."
  workedExample="s1 = 'bosh auto', s2 = 'bosch auto'. d_L = 1 (insert 'c'). Max length = 10. Sim = 1 - (1/10) = 0.90 (90% match, exceeds threshold 0.85 -> flags as duplicate candidate)."
  interpretation="Automated deduplication prevents creating duplicate vendor records for the same legal supplier, protecting corporate negotiating power."
/>

# 06 — CASES & APPLICATIONS: Subway Global Ingredient Harmonization

* **Enterprise Scope**: Subway operates over 37,000 franchised sandwich restaurants across 100+ countries.
* **The Legacy Crisis**:
  * Historically, regional franchise cooperatives and individual store owners purchased supplies through localized distributors.
  * Identical raw ingredients (e.g., sliced jalapeños, wheat flour, napkins) were coded under hundreds of proprietary vendor item numbers.
  * Corporate purchasing lacked enterprise-wide visibility: negotiating global supply contracts with tomato growers or paper manufacturers was impossible because corporate could not determine its total global tomato consumption.
* **The Master Data Transformation**:
  * Established an enterprise-wide Master Data Governance (MDG) hub.
  * Every ingredient was cataloged under a single Global Material Master SKU (e.g., `MAT-ING-JAL-001` for standard pickled sliced jalapeños).
  * Enforced standardized recipe Bills of Materials (BOM) linked directly to point-of-sale (POS) cash registers.
* **Quantifiable Business Impact**:
  * Consolidated global purchasing unlocked an estimated $140M in annual volume pricing discounts.
  * Food safety traceability: In the event of an agricultural pathogen outbreak, corporate can trace the exact farm lot number of any ingredient across all 37,000 stores within 90 seconds.
  * Restaurant ingredient stockout rates plummeted from 2.4% to under 0.2%.
