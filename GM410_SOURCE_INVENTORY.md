## 3. Examination Question Inventory (2023–2025)

- **Format**: Total 60 Marks. Q1: Attempt any 5 of 7 (6 Marks each = 30M). Q2: Compulsory Case (30 Marks).

- **Q1(c) [6M]**: Balanced Scorecard (BSC) with reference to business strategy. Two leading and two lagging indicators across four perspectives.
- **Q1(d) [6M]**: What is A/B testing? Explain the three key principles of good Design of Experiments (Fisher: Randomisation, Replication, Blocking).
- **Q1(e) [6M]**: Retail app collecting purchases, browsing, location, and device data for targeted ads and predictive analytics. What ethical considerations must be addressed? (Consent, purpose limitation, transparency, data minimization, security, non-discrimination).
- **Q1(f) [6M]**: PESTEL for a new wearable fitness device, and how to handle tariff impacts. (6 dimensions, tariff mitigation via CKD/SKD assembly, supply chain diversification, price elasticity).
- **Q1(g) [6M]**: Two key challenges during technology transformation. (Cultural resistance & skills gap; legacy architecture integration & data silo fragmentation).

## 5. Academic Reading List Inventory (Section C)

1. **Roger Martin & Hilary Austen (1999)**: *The Art of Integrative Thinking*. 4-stage cognitive model: Salience (which variables matter), Causality (how variables interact), Sequencing/Architecture (where to cut into the problem), Resolution (managing creative tensions without compromise).
2. **Thompson, Peteraf, Gamble & Strickland (19th Ed.)**: *Crafting and Executing Strategy*. Five-stage strategy process; Three tests of a winning strategy (Good Fit, Competitive Advantage, Strong Performance); Five generic competitive strategies.
3. **Michael Porter (1980)**: *Competitive Strategy: Techniques for Analyzing Industries and Competitors*. Structural analysis of industries; Five Forces; Competitor response profiles.
4. **Michael Porter (HBR 1996)**: *What Is Strategy?*. Operational Effectiveness (OE) vs Strategic Positioning; the Productivity Frontier; Trade-offs create sustainable advantage; Three types of Strategic Fit (First-order consistency, Second-order reinforcing, Third-order optimization).
5. **Michael Porter (HBS 2008)**: *Understanding Industry Structure*. Root causes of Five Forces; cyclical vs structural changes.
6. **Pankaj Ghemawat & Jan Rivkin (HBS 1998)**: *Creating Competitive Advantage*. Added value; Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) vs Cost Wedge; activity systems.
7. **Todd Jick (HBS 1989)**: *The Vision Thing*. Seven qualities of an effective vision; vision deployment and organizational buy-in.
8. **Andrew Winston (2018)**: *Why Mining, Yes, Mining, Cares About Sustainability*. Integrating ESG into heavy extractive industries; license to operate; long-term resource efficiency.
