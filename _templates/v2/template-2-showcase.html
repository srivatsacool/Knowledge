02. Three-Tier Client-Server Architecture

Three-Tier Architecture physically and logically decouples computing into three discrete operational layers: Presentation Tier (Desktop/Mobile GUI), Application Logic Tier (Business Rules & Dispatcher), and Database Tier (Central Relational DB).

🚨 Exam Trap: Network Latency!

Students frequently confuse 3-Tier with 2-Tier. In 3-Tier, client PCs never execute SQL directly against the database; all database queries pass strictly through Tier-2 application servers to prevent client-side security breaches and connection pool saturation.

04. Mathematical Inventory Formulation

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) Model

S

05. Model Exam Answer (10 Marks)

FAQ #01 10 Marks Standard ⏱ 12 Minutes

"Explain ERP as both a technology strategy and business strategy."

Marking Rubric: Technology Strategy (3 Marks) + Business Strategy (3 Marks) + Interlocking Mechanism (4 Marks).

1. Technology Strategy (3 Marks): As an IT strategy, ERP eliminates siloed departmental databases by establishing a single centralized relational database schema. It standardizes communication across all operating entities and provides a 3-tier client-server foundation.

2. Business Strategy (3 Marks): As a corporate strategy, ERP mandates Business Process Re-engineering (BPR). It eliminates departmental friction, aligns operational execution with strategic KPIs, and provides executive leadership with real-time auditability.

3. Interlocking Mechanism (4 Marks): The technology infrastructure provides transaction horsepower, while the business strategy directs what data must flow and how decisions are made. A failure to align both results in automated inefficiency.
