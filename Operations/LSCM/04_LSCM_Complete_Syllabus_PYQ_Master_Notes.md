* **Course Outcome:** CO3 | **Responsible Faculty:** Prof. Ajit M. Maurya
* **Exam Importance:** 🔥 **MUST MASTER** | **Likely Question Forms:** 10M Comparative Analysis, 5M Concept Note, 2M Fill-in-the-Blank.
* **Core Concepts & Frameworks:**
  * *SCM Historical Evolution:* 1970s Operational Silos &rarr; 1990s Internal ERP Integration &rarr; 2020s Extended Value-Chain Ecosystems.
  * *The Strategic Triad:* Balancing Cost Efficiency, Market Responsiveness, and Systemic Resilience.
  * *The Six Foundational Supply Chain Drivers:* Logistical (Facilities, Inventory, Transportation) and Cross-Functional (Information, Sourcing, Pricing).
  * *Marshall Fisher’s Strategic Fit Matrix:* Functional Products (stable, low margin) require **Physically Efficient Chains**; Innovative Products (volatile, high margin) require **Market-Responsive Chains**. Misalignment creates severe stockouts or uncompetitive landed costs.

  * *Financial Velocity Metrics:* Inventory Turnover Ratio ($ITR = \text{COGS} / \text{Avg Inventory}$), Days of Supply ($DOS = 365 / ITR$), Cash-to-Cash Cycle ($C2C = DIO + DSO - DPO$), On-Time In-Full ($OTIF$).
* **Enterprise Benchmarks:** Walmart & P&G (Efficient Supply Chain); Zara & Apple (Responsive Supply Chain).
* **Related Past Examination Questions:** 2023 Q1.4 (Responsive SC), 2023 Q2 (High-Tech Trade-off), 2024 Q4.e (Logistics Cost Elements).

### Topic 8.0: Managing Uncertainty, Safety Stock & Stochastic Newsvendor Model
* **Course Outcome:** CO1 | **Responsible Faculty:** Prof. Praful More
* **Exam Importance:** 🧮 **NUMERICAL PRACTICE** | **Likely Question Forms:** 10M Numerical Problem, 5M Derivation.
* **Core Concepts & Frameworks:**
  * *Safety Stock Formulations:*
    * Demand Variable: $SS = z \cdot \sigma_d \sqrt{L}$.
    * Lead Time Variable: $SS = z \cdot d \cdot \sigma_L$.
    * Dual Uncertainty: $SS = z \cdot \sqrt{L \cdot \sigma_d^2 + d^2 \cdot \sigma_L^2}$.
    * Reorder Point: $ROP = (d \cdot L) + SS$.
  * *The Newsvendor Model:* $C_u = P - C, C_o = C - S, CR = C_u / (C_u + C_o), Q^* = \mu + z \cdot \sigma$.
  * *Double Marginalization Dilemma:* Independent store managers under-order due to $CR_{\text{retailer}} < CR_{\text{chain}}$.
* **Related Past Examination Questions:** 2025 Q1.b (Newsvendor Umbrella, 5M), 2025 Q1.c (Continuous Review Vaccine, 5M).

### Question 2: Analytical Trade-Off & EOQ Calculation (10 Marks)
* **(a) Analytical Trade-Off (5 Marks):**
  High-tech electronics feature rapid technological depreciation (1–2% value loss per month). Air freight carries a premium transport cost but compresses transit lead time from 35 days (ocean) to 3 days, reducing safety and pipeline inventory by over 90% and eliminating obsolete inventory write-offs. Ocean freight lowers freight spend but requires massive pipeline buffers that risk catastrophic obsolescence.
* **(b) Step-by-Step Math (5 Marks):**
  * *Parameters:* $D = 10,000 \text{ units/yr}, S = \$10/\text{order}, C = \$10, H = \$5.00/\text{unit/yr}, \text{Freight} = \$10.00/\text{unit}$.
  * *EOQ:* $EOQ = \sqrt{(2 \times 10,000 \times 10) / 5} = \sqrt{40,000} = \mathbf{200 \text{ units}}$.
  * *Order Frequency:* $N^* = 10,000 / 200 = \mathbf{50 \text{ orders per year}}$.
  * *Annual Ordering Cost:* $50 \times \$10 = \$500.00$.
  * *Annual Holding Cost:* $(200 / 2) \times \$5.00 = \$500.00$.
  * *Total Inventory Cost:* $\$500 + \$500 = \mathbf{\$1,000.00/\text{year}}$.
  * *Total Transportation Cost:* $10,000 \times \$10.00 = \mathbf{\$100,000.00/\text{year}}$.
  * *Total Annual Logistics Cost:* $\$1,000.00 + \$100,000.00 = \mathbf{\$101,000.00/\text{year}}$.

### Question 3: ABC Pharma COVID-19 Resiliency Case (10 Marks)
* **Core Problem:** Sole-source dependence on a single Wuhan chemical synthesis plant for critical API.
* **Diagnostic ($TTR$ vs. $TTS$):** $TTS \approx 25 \text{ days}$ (on-hand safety stock) vs. $TTR \approx 120 \text{ days}$ (quarantine, production restart, export air clearances). Because $TTR \gg TTS$, catastrophic stockouts occurred.
* **Resilience Framework:**
  1. *Dual-Sourcing Architecture (70/30 Rule):* 70% primary volume with cost-effective scale supplier; 30% baseline volume contracted to a qualified nearshore domestic API manufacturer.
  2. *Strategic Buffer Stocks:* Maintain an emergency 60-day buffer of high-purity API inventory stored in temperature-controlled regional bonded warehouses.
  3. *Multi-Tier Digital Mapping:* Real-time tracking of Tier-1 and Tier-2 precursor chemical facilities.
  4. *Regulatory Pre-Approval:* Pre-qualify alternate chemical manufacturing sites with the US FDA / CDSCO to allow instantaneous volume transfer without regulatory delays.

* **(a) Byproduct Synergy:** Industrial symbiosis where waste from one production process becomes the feedstock for another (e.g., steel furnace slag used in cement manufacturing).

### Question 2: ElectroTech Solutions ABC Inventory Redesign (15 Marks)
* **(a) Differentiated Inventory Policies (5 Marks):**

  * *Order Frequency:* $N^* = 50,000 / 2,236.07 \approx \mathbf{22.36 \text{ orders per year}}$.
  * *Annual Holding Cost:* $(2,236.07 / 2) \times \$2.00 = \mathbf{\$2,236.07/\text{year}}$.
* **(c) Continuous Review $(r, Q)$ Pros & Cons (5 Marks):**
  * *Pros:* Orders triggered immediately when stock hits $r$; requires lower safety stock because stockout exposure is limited strictly to lead time $L$ rather than $R + L$.
  * *Cons:* Requires expensive real-time IT, barcode/RFID scanning at every stock movement, and high administrative order processing overhead.

### Question 3: OptiFresh Perishable Distribution Route Optimization (15 Marks)
* **Current Dilemma:** 120 retail stores served via once-daily morning runs from a central DC. Midday stockouts of perishable produce cause lost sales. Shifting to twice-daily uniform runs would increase logistics costs by 65% and accelerate cold-chain temperature degradation.
* **Proposed Solution (Hybrid Clustering Model):**
  1. *Velocity Segmentation:* Tier 1 (35 high-volume downtown supermarkets) receives twice-daily dynamic replenishment; Tier 2 (85 standard neighborhood grocers) maintains once-daily morning deliveries scheduled via VRP software.
  2. *Periphery Micro-Cross-Docks:* Inbound refrigerated line-haul trucks feed two peripheral cross-docks at 11:00 AM, where smaller electric delivery vans execute afternoon top-up runs.
  3. *Result:* Eradicates 95% of midday stockouts while limiting overall logistics cost increases to only 14%.

### Question 1: Three-Part Quantitative Inventory Mastery (15 Marks | 3 &times; 5M)
* **(a) Deterministic EOQ:**
  $$D = 5,000, \quad S = \$40, \quad H = \$2.00$$
  $$EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 5,000 \times 40}{2}} = \sqrt{200,000} \approx \mathbf{447.21 \implies 447 \text{ units}}$$
  $$TC^* = \left(\frac{5,000}{447.21}\right)40 + \left(\frac{447.21}{2}\right)2.00 = \$447.21 + \$447.21 = \mathbf{\$894.43/\text{year}}$$
* **(b) Stochastic Single-Period (Newsvendor):**
  $$P = \$30, \quad C = \$15, \quad S = \$10$$
  $$C_u = P - C = \$30 - \$15 = \mathbf{\$15.00}, \quad C_o = C - S = \$15 - \$10 = \mathbf{\$5.00}$$
  $$CR = \frac{C_u}{C_u + C_o} = \frac{15}{15 + 5} = \frac{15}{20} = \mathbf{0.75 \implies 75\%}$$
  Looking up standard normal distribution $\Phi(z) = 0.7500 \implies \mathbf{z = 0.674}$.
* **(c) Multi-Period Continuous Review:**
  $$s = 200, \quad S = 500, \quad \mu_{DL} = 150, \quad \sigma_{DL} = 30$$
  $$SS = s - \mu_{DL} = 200 - 150 = \mathbf{50 \text{ doses}}$$
  $$z = \frac{SS}{\sigma_{DL}} = \frac{50}{30} = \mathbf{1.667 \approx 1.67}$$
  $$\text{Cycle Service Level (CSL)} = \Phi(1.67) = \mathbf{0.9525 \implies 95.25\%}$$
