## 2. Topic Recurrence & Frequency Classification

### High Frequency (Every Single Year — 100% Probability)
1. **Deterministic Inventory Models (EOQ, EPQ, Quantity Discounts)**:
   - *2023*: Calculate EOQ, optimal number of orders, and annual total cost under given holding percentage. (10 Marks)
   - *2024*: Complex EOQ numerical with supplier discount tiers (All-units discount at 1,000 and 2,500 units). (10 Marks)
   - *2025*: EPQ problem with finite production rate ($p = 200$ units/day, $d = 50$ units/day). (10 Marks)
2. **Fisher's Strategic Fit & Implied Demand Uncertainty**:
   - *2023*: Explain Fisher's model. Classify an apparel retailer vs. a generic pharmaceutical firm. (10 Marks)
   - *2024*: Analyze the failure of a company attempting to supply innovative products via an efficient supply chain. (10 Marks)
   - *2025*: Detail the steps to achieve strategic fit between competitive strategy and supply chain drivers. (10 Marks)
3. **Stochastic Safety Stock & Service Levels**:
   - *2023*: Calculate Safety Stock and Reorder Point for 95% service level given standard deviation of lead-time demand. (10 Marks)
   - *2024*: Safety stock calculation under uncertain lead time ($L = 10 \pm 2$ days). (10 Marks)
   - *2025*: Compare Continuous Review $(s, Q)$ with Periodic Review $(R, S)$ policy under stockout penalties. (10 Marks)
4. **Warehouse Layout & Cross-Docking Operations**:
   - *2023*: Differentiate Distribution Center (DC) and Fulfillment Center (FC). Draw standard cross-docking flow. (10 Marks)
   - *2024*: Design an optimal layout for an omnichannel retailer warehouse. Explain ASRS and picking systems. (10 Marks)
   - *2025*: Step-by-step physical flow from inbound unloading to put-away, wave picking, and outbound loading. (10 Marks)

   - *2024*: Describe the 5 steps of the monthly S&OP consensus process. (10 Marks)

## 3. Verbatim Exam Archive (2023, 2024, 2025)

### Examination 2023
- **Q1 (Case Analysis, 20 Marks)**:
  - *(a)* A consumer goods conglomerate operates 14 regional warehouses across India. Rising transport freight and inventory holding costs have depressed operating margin. Evaluate the pros and cons of consolidating into 4 central mega-distribution centers. (12 Marks)
  - *(b)* Outline the trade-offs between inventory centralization and transport transit time. (8 Marks)
- **Q2 (Numerical Inventory, 10 Marks)**:
  - Annual demand for industrial ball bearings $D = 12,000$ units. Ordering cost $S = \text{₹}1,500$ per order. Inventory carrying cost is $20\%$ of purchase price per annum. Unit purchase price $C = \text{₹}250$.
  - Compute: (i) Economic Order Quantity, (ii) Number of orders per year, (iii) Cycle time in weeks (assume 50 work weeks/year), (iv) Total annual inventory management cost.
- **Q3 (Strategic Framework, 10 Marks)**:
  - Discuss Marshall Fisher's model of matching supply chains with products. Illustrate why innovative products require responsive supply chains and functional products demand efficient supply chains. Provide Indian corporate examples.
- **Q4 (Stochastic Safety Stock, 10 Marks)**:
  - Daily demand for an electronic component is normally distributed with mean $\mu_d = 80$ units and standard deviation $\sigma_d = 12$ units. Replenishment lead time is constant at $L = 9$ days.
  - Determine: (i) Reorder Point for a $95\%$ Cycle Service Level ($Z = 1.645$), (ii) Safety stock held, (iii) If management increases service level to $99\%$ ($Z = 2.326$), calculate the percentage increase in safety stock.
- **Q5 (Warehousing Operations, 10 Marks)**:
  - Describe the core operational processes of a modern distribution warehouse: Receiving, Put-away, Storage, Picking, Packing, and Dispatch. Highlight the key differences between batch picking and zone picking.
- **Q6 (Cross-Docking & Unitisation, 10 Marks)**:
  - What is cross-docking? Under what operational and technological conditions does cross-docking succeed? Distinguish between Pre-distribution and Post-distribution cross-docking.
- **Q7 (Transportation Infrastructure, 10 Marks)**:
  - Elaborate on the economic drivers of transportation: Economies of Scale and Economies of Distance (the Tapering Principle). How does transport mode selection impact inventory holding cost?
- **Q8 (Global Trade & INCOTERMS, 10 Marks)**:
  - Explain the purpose of ICC INCOTERMS 2020. Select any three terms (one each from F, C, and D groups) and explain the exact division of costs, freight responsibilities, and point of risk transfer between buyer and seller.

### Examination 2024
- **Q1 (Case Analysis, 20 Marks)**:
  - Fast-Fashion apparel retail chain "Z-Style" experiences extreme stockouts of trendy lines while accumulating surplus stock of standard denim.
  - *(a)* Diagnose the supply chain root cause using the Implied Demand Uncertainty framework. (10 Marks)
  - *(b)* Recommend operational interventions across Sourcing, Manufacturing, and Logistics to achieve Strategic Fit. (10 Marks)
- **Q2 (Quantity Discount Numerical, 10 Marks)**:
  - An automotive OEM requires $D = 24,000$ radiator assemblies annually. Setup cost $S = \text{₹}3,600$ per order. Inventory holding cost fraction $i = 25\%$ of unit price per year.
  - The supplier offers the following all-units discount price schedule:
    - Order Quantity $1 \le Q < 1,000$: Unit Price $\text{₹}600$
    - Order Quantity $1,000 \le Q < 2,500$: Unit Price $\text{₹}580$
    - Order Quantity $Q \ge 2,500$: Unit Price $\text{₹}560$
  - Determine the economically optimal order quantity $Q^*$ that minimizes total annual acquisition and inventory cost.
- **Q3 (Supply Chain Drivers & SCOR, 10 Marks)**:
  - *(a)* Detail the 6 supply chain drivers (Facilities, Inventory, Transportation, Information, Sourcing, Pricing) and their roles in determining supply chain responsiveness versus efficiency. (6 Marks)
  - *(b)* Briefly explain the SCOR Model Level-1 processes: Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return, Enable. (4 Marks)
- **Q4 (S&OP & Planning Execution, 10 Marks)**:
  - Explain the 5-step monthly Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) cycle. How does S&OP differ from weekly Sales & Operations Execution (S&OE)?
- **Q5 (Lead Time Uncertainty Numerical, 10 Marks)**:
  - An industrial distributor faces daily demand with mean $d = 150$ units and $\sigma_d = 20$ units. Supplier lead time is stochastic with mean $L = 16$ days and standard deviation $\sigma_L = 3$ days.
  - Compute the required safety stock and reorder point for a $98\%$ service level ($Z = 2.054$). Explain the relative impact of demand variance versus lead-time variance on safety stock buffer size.
- **Q6 (Warehouse Layout & Automation, 10 Marks)**:
  - Draw and explain an optimal layout for an e-commerce Fulfillment Center handling high SKU counts. Contrast traditional manual selective racking with Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems (AS/RS).
- **Q7 (Multimodal Freight & Green Logistics, 10 Marks)**:
  - Discuss the operational and environmental trade-offs between Road, Rail, Coastal Shipping, and Air freight in India. What role does the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan play in multi-modal integration?
- **Q8 (Short Notes — 2 × 5 = 10 Marks)**:
  - Attempt any two:
    - *(a)* INCOTERMS 2020: DDP vs. EXW
    - *(b)* Third-Party Logistics (3PL) vs. Fourth-Party Logistics (4PL)
    - *(c)* Theory of Constraints: Drum-Buffer-Rope mechanism in logistics

### Examination 2025
- **Q1 (Comprehensive SCM Case, 20 Marks)**:
  - A pharmaceutical firm transitioning from generic medicines to patented biotech drugs encounters severe fulfillment disruptions.
  - *(a)* Compare the supply chain architectures required for generic commodities versus high-value biotech therapeutics. (10 Marks)
  - *(b)* Detail how the company should restructure its inventory holding points (centralized vs. decentralized) and cold-chain transportation. (10 Marks)
- **Q2 (EPQ Numerical with Finite Production Rate, 10 Marks)**:
  - A manufacturing facility produces packaging cartons. Annual demand $D = 50,000$ units. The machine production rate $p = 500$ units/day. The facility operates 250 working days per year (daily demand rate $d = 200$ units/day). Setup cost $S = \text{₹}2,500$ per production run. Annual holding cost $H = \text{₹}10$ per unit per year.
  - Calculate: (i) Economic Production Quantity (EPQ), (ii) Maximum inventory level reached, (iii) Total annual setup and holding cost, (iv) Production run length in days.
- **Q3 (Demand Forecasting & CPFR, 10 Marks)**:
  - Explain the 9-step Collaborative Planning, Forecasting, and Replenishment (CPFR) model. How does CPFR systematically eliminate the Bullwhip Effect?
- **Q4 (Planning & Cross-Docking, 10 Marks)**:
  - *(a)* Aggregate Planning strategies: Compare Level Strategy, Chase Strategy, and Mixed Strategy with workforce and cost implications. (5 Marks)
  - *(b)* Discuss the technological prerequisites for successful real-time cross-docking. (5 Marks)
- **Q5 (Periodic vs. Continuous Review Inventory Policies, 10 Marks)**:
  - Compare Continuous Review $(s, Q)$ policy and Periodic Review $(R, S)$ policy. Derive the Target Stock Level formula for periodic review and explain in what retail contexts each policy is superior.
- **Q6 (Warehouse Material Handling & Order Picking, 10 Marks)**:
  - Compare the throughput capacity, error rates, and equipment investment for: Discrete Order Picking, Batch Picking, Zone Picking, and Wave Picking. When is Pick-to-Light or Voice Picking justifiable?
- **Q7 (Logistics Network Design & Gravity Location Model, 10 Marks)**:
  - Explain how the Center of Gravity (Grid) method is used to determine the optimal geographical coordinates for a central distribution center. What are the practical limitations of the gravity model in real-world road networks?
- **Q8 (Strategic SCM & Sustainability, 10 Marks)**:
  - *(a)* The role of 4PL supply chain integrators in managing end-to-end global supply networks. (5 Marks)
  - *(b)* Reverse logistics and closed-loop supply chains: Discuss the 5 R's (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Remanufacture, Recover) with an electronics industry case. (5 Marks)
