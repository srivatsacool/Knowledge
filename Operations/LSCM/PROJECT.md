# Logistics & Supply Chain Management: Value Creation, Adaptability & Sustainability
**WeSchool PGDM — Trimester IV | Course Code: LSCM-T4 | 3 Credits (15 Sessions)**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Pedagogical Scope

Logistics and Supply Chain Management (LSCM) is the core operational backbone connecting corporate strategy, network design, multi-echelon inventory control, material handling, and end-to-end customer fulfillment. In modern volatile environments (marked by supply disruptions, demand shocks, and rapid omnichannel scaling), high-performing firms compete as **extended supply networks** rather than isolated commercial entities.

This knowledge project integrates the full 15-session Trimester IV curriculum across three distinct faculty perspectives, validated against three consecutive academic years of past university examinations (2023, 2024, and 2025):

1. **Strategic & System Architecture**: Prof. Ajit Maurya (Strategic Fit, Drivers, Transportation Economics, Logistics Costing, S&OP/S&OE, 3PL/4PL).
2. **Operational Physical Execution**: Prof. Manoj (DC vs. FC layout mechanics, receiving/put-away, cross-docking, material handling, warehouse KPI math).
3. **Mathematical Modeling & Inventory Control**: Prof. Praful More (Deterministic EOQ/EPQ, stochastic safety stock with lead-time variability, newsboy perishable models, replenishment policies).

## 4. Exam Frequency & Topic Prioritization

Based on verified OCR extraction of past examination papers (2023–2025):

- **Tier A (Recurring Every Year — 100% Probability)**:
  1. *Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)*: Numerical calculation + sensitivity analysis + discount tiers (10–12 marks).
  2. *Safety Stock & Reorder Point (ROP)*: Stochastic lead-time uncertainty formulations (8–10 marks).
  3. *Fisher's Strategic Fit Model*: Functional vs. Innovative goods matched to Efficient vs. Responsive chains (8–10 marks).
  4. *Warehouse Process Optimization*: Cross-docking vs. traditional storage; Receiving to Dispatch layout (10 marks).
- **Tier B (High-Frequency Tactical Topics — 70% Probability)**:
  1. *Supply Chain Drivers*: Interplay of Facility, Inventory, Transportation, Information, Sourcing, and Pricing.
  2. *Transportation Cost Economics*: Economies of scale (weight/volume) vs. Economies of distance (tapering principle).
  3. *Total Cost Concept*: Trade-offs between freight costs, inventory holding costs, and customer stockout penalties.
  4. *Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP)*: Monthly collaborative planning sequence vs. weekly tactical S&OE execution.
- **Tier C (Specialized Theory & Conceptual Frameworks — 40% Probability)**:
  1. 3PL vs. 4PL orchestrator roles.
  2. Reverse logistics closed-loop design and sustainability metrics.
  3. Theory of Constraints (TOC) Drum-Buffer-Rope mechanism.
