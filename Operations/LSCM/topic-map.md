## Faculty-Specific Pedagogical Signatures

### 1. Prof. Ajit Maurya (System Strategy & Logistics Networks)
- **Signature Emphasis**: Macro-level supply chain architectures, trade-off equilibrium, strategic alignment, and network modeling.
- **Key Frameworks**:
  - Implied Demand Uncertainty Frontier (Chopra-Fisher Model).
  - 6 Drivers Matrix (Facilities, Inventory, Transportation, Information, Sourcing, Pricing).
  - Tapering Transportation Principle (Freight Rate per Ton-KM decreases as distance increases).
  - Total Logistics Cost Curve (Inventory Carrying Cost vs. Transport Freight vs. Order Processing).
  - S&OP Monthly Closed-Loop Consensus Process.

### 2. Prof. Manoj (Physical Warehouse Flow & Material Logistics)
- **Signature Emphasis**: Floor-level logistics engineering, dock operations, material handling equipment (MHE), and warehouse productivity KPIs.
- **Key Frameworks**:
  - DC vs. FC Functional Divergence (Pallet-in/Pallet-out vs. Each-in/Each-out).
  - 6 Warehouse Steps: Receiving $\to$ Pre-sorting $\to$ Put-away $\to$ Storage $\to$ Order Picking $\to$ Packing & Dispatch.
  - Cross-Docking Decoupling Architecture (Pure flow-through vs. merge-in-transit).
  - Storage Rack Configurations (Selective Pallet Racking, Drive-in, Push-back, AS/RS).
  - Order Picking Topologies: Discrete Picking, Batch Picking, Zone Picking, Wave Picking.

### 3. Prof. Praful More (Mathematical Modeling & Inventory Optimization)
- **Signature Emphasis**: Quantitative rigor, algebraic derivations, stochastic probability modeling, and numerical accuracy.
- **Key Frameworks**:
  - Ford W. Harris EOQ First-Order Derivative Proof ($\frac{dTC}{dQ} = -\frac{DS}{Q^2} + \frac{H}{2} = 0$).
  - Economic Production Quantity (EPQ) with simultaneous production-consumption rate ($p > d$).
  - Multi-tier Quantity Discount Optimization (All-units vs. Incremental).
  - Safety Stock under Dual Variance ($\sigma_{DL} = \sqrt{L \sigma_d^2 + d^2 \sigma_L^2}$).
  - Newsboy Marginal Analysis for Perishable Inventory ($P(d \le Q^*) = \frac{C_u}{C_u + C_o}$).
